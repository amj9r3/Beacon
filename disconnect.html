Disconnect Beacon

Disconnecting ends Beacon's access to your QuickBooks Online company. You can do this two ways:

From QuickBooks Online

  • Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  • Go to Settings → Apps (or Account → Connected apps).
  • Find Beacon and choose Disconnect.

Intuit revokes the authorization immediately and Beacon can no longer reach your company data.

From Beacon

Beacon's own disconnect step revokes its stored authorization token and removes the local connection to your QuickBooks Online company, so it can no longer make requests on your behalf.

After disconnecting, any data Beacon previously retained on your systems remains under your control; delete it directly if you no longer want it.
