How to opt in

Beacon is a private, non-promotional administrative assistant operated by Aaron Jones. It is offered only to family members and advisers Aaron knows personally.

To opt in, text START to (254) 505-3742.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring administrative text messages and phone calls, including calls that use an AI-generated voice, from Beacon (Aaron Jones) at the number you texted from. Messages cover answers to your questions, meeting and appointment confirmations, reminders you request, document-coordination notices, and status updates on your requests. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of texts; you can also ask to stop calls at any time.

After you text START, Beacon replies: “Beacon (Aaron Jones): You are subscribed to Beacon administrative texts and calls. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Terms: https://amj9r3.github.io/Beacon/terms.html”

Beacon does not text or call a number that has not opted in, and it does not add anyone to a group conversation without that person’s own opt-in.

Your choices

Opting in is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing anything. Reply STOP to cancel; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. For help, reply HELP or email idealimagellc@gmail.com.

We do not sell or share mobile numbers or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
