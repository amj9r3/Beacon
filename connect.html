Connect to QuickBooks Online

Beacon connects to its owner's QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization. The owner starts the flow, signs in to Intuit, and grants the requested scopes; Intuit returns an authorization code that Beacon exchanges for access tokens.

Reconnect

If the connection lapses or tokens expire, the same authorization flow re-establishes access. No other party is ever involved.

How it runs. The operator starts the authorization from Beacon. Intuit's sign-in opens, the operator approves the requested scopes, and Beacon stores the returned tokens locally to make subsequent requests. No other party is involved at any step.
