Beacon Private instance

Single-operator financial agent

One signal, kept by one operator.

Beacon is a private software agent that connects to its owner's own QuickBooks Online company to help organize and review personal financial data. It is built and run by one person, for that one person. There are no other users, no public sign-up, and nothing for sale.

What it is

Beacon runs on infrastructure the owner controls. It reads financial records from the owner's connected QuickBooks Online company so the owner can see, summarize, and reason about their own books in one place. It is not a product offered to the public and does not act on behalf of anyone but its owner.

What it accesses

  • Connects toThe owner's own QuickBooks Online company, by OAuth 2.0 authorization.
  • ReadsAccounting records (e.g. accounts, transactions, balances) from that company.
  • StoresData the owner chooses to retain, on the owner's own systems.
  • SharesNothing. No data is sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.
  • UsersOne — the owner. No accounts are created for anyone else.

Access & control

The connection to QuickBooks Online is established by the owner and can be revoked at any time, either from within Intuit's account settings or through the disconnect page. Revoking ends Beacon's access immediately.

Scope of use. Because Beacon is a private instance with a single operator, its terms and privacy practices describe one person's handling of their own data. Read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for the full statement.
