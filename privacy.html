Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Beacon software agent ("Beacon," "the app") handles data. Beacon is a private application operated by Aaron Jones ("the operator") for the operator's sole personal use. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the operator.

1. Who this covers

Beacon has a single user: its operator. It does not create accounts for, collect data from, or provide services to any other person. Where this policy refers to "your data," it means the operator's own data.

2. What data Beacon accesses

With the operator's authorization through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 flow, Beacon accesses accounting data from the operator's own QuickBooks Online company. Depending on the scopes the operator grants, this may include accounts, transactions, balances, and related financial records. Beacon accesses this data to help the operator review and organize their own books.

3. How data is used

Data accessed through QuickBooks Online is used only to provide the operator with summaries, analysis, and organization of their own financial information. It is not used for advertising, profiling of other people, training of third-party models, or any purpose beyond the operator's personal financial management.

4. Storage and security

Any data Beacon retains is stored on systems controlled by the operator. Access credentials (such as OAuth tokens) are stored in restricted, owner-only files and are not embedded in documents, source code, or shared channels. The operator is responsible for the security of the systems on which Beacon runs.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Beacon does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose data to third parties. No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Data may be processed by infrastructure the operator chooses to use (for example, local hardware the operator controls), solely to operate the app for the operator.

6. Retention and deletion

The operator decides what data Beacon retains and for how long, and may delete it at any time. Revoking Beacon's QuickBooks Online connection (see Section 7) stops further access.

7. Revoking access

The operator can disconnect Beacon from QuickBooks Online at any time through Intuit's account settings (Apps → Connected apps) or via the disconnect page. Revocation ends Beacon's access to the QuickBooks Online company immediately.

8. Children

Beacon is a private tool for an adult operator and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

If the operator changes how Beacon handles data, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to idealimagellc@gmail.com.
